# Ghanaian Cultural Lexicon (GCL)

   [![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.22017886.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22017886)

## An Open Resource for Ghanaian Language, Culture, and AI

The **Ghanaian Cultural Lexicon (GCL)** is an open, structured, and community-validated resource documenting Ghanaian languages, cultural concepts, expressions, practices, and their contextual meanings.

The project aims to preserve Ghanaian cultural knowledge while making it accessible for **research, education, computational linguistics, natural language processing (NLP), artificial intelligence (AI), and future culturally-aware technologies**.

##  Why GCL?

Ghana has a rich and diverse linguistic and cultural heritage. However, much of this knowledge exists in oral traditions, community practices, local expressions, proverbs, indigenous terminology, and culturally specific meanings that are not adequately represented in digital datasets.

GCL seeks to help address this gap by creating a structured and continuously evolving resource that documents:

- Ghanaian words and expressions
- Indigenous cultural concepts
- Proverbs and idioms
- Kinship and social relationships
- Traditional institutions
- Festivals and ceremonies
- Food and cuisine
- Clothing and symbols
- Ghanaian English
- Ghanaian Pidgin
- Cultural and psychological concepts
- Regional and linguistic variation


##  Objectives

The project aims to:

1. **Document** Ghanaian linguistic and cultural knowledge.
2. **Preserve** culturally significant concepts for future generations.
3. **Represent** Ghanaian cultural contexts in digital datasets.
4. **Support** research in linguistics, psychology, anthropology, sociology, and related fields.
5. **Support AI/NLP research** involving Ghanaian languages and cultural contexts.
6. **Encourage community participation** in documenting and validating cultural knowledge.

## Language and Cultural Coverage

The project is designed to represent Ghana's linguistic and cultural diversity.

Initial work focuses on **Twi/Akan**, with future expansion planned across Ghanaian languages and varieties including:

- Akan/Twi
- Fante
- Ga
- Ewe
- Dagbani
- Dagaare
- Gonja
- Gurene
- Nzema
- Ghanaian English
- Ghanaian Pidgin
- Other Ghanaian languages and varieties

The project does **not** assume that a cultural meaning found in one community automatically applies to all Ghanaians.

Regional and community differences are therefore documented where possible.


##  Cultural Context

GCL is more than a translation dictionary.

An entry may document:

> **Term → Literal Meaning → Cultural Meaning → Context → Example → Region → Related Concepts**

This allows the dataset to preserve meanings that may be lost through direct translation.

Where psychological or theoretical interpretations are included, these are distinguished from the linguistic or cultural description of the term.

##  Validation

Entries may move through several stages:

**Proposed → Under Review → Validated**

An entry should not be treated as authoritative simply because it appears in the dataset.

Where meanings are disputed, regional, or uncertain, the dataset should preserve that uncertainty rather than forcing a single definition.
